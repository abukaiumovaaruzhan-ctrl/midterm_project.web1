WHO WE ARE

PLAY. IMPROVE. CONNECT.

SpinPoint is a table tennis club created for people who enjoy an active lifestyle and want to improve their game.

Our club welcomes beginners, intermediate players and experienced athletes. You can come for training, friendly matches or simply to meet other players.
